# Code of Conduct

OilSignal follows a simple contributor standard: be respectful, specific, and focused on the work. Harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing, and sustained disruption are not acceptable in project spaces.

Project maintainers may remove content or restrict participation when behavior undermines a safe, productive technical community. Good-faith disagreement about architecture, data quality, licensing, market interpretation, or product direction is welcome when it stays evidence-based and civil.

For sensitive conduct reports, use the private contact method listed in the repository's security/contact settings rather than opening a public issue.
