# OilSignal founding pilot: buyer-validation kit

Status: **commercial hypothesis; not an announced price or customer commitment**. This kit is for founder review and prospective conversations. No outreach, invoicing, payment setup, or contract is authorized by this document.

## Decision for this experiment

Sell **one repeated weekly petroleum evidence workflow** to a small petroleum research/consulting team. Do not pitch a general oil dashboard or ask buyers to replace their current process.

**Primary candidate:** independent petroleum consultant or 2–20-person energy research/advisory team that publishes a weekly client-facing note. **Secondary candidate:** downstream fuel supply/procurement team.

The consultancy wedge is a hypothesis, not observed demand. It differs from the repository's Tier-1 operator ICP and must earn its priority in buyer conversations. For a consultant, choose **Crude Flow Reconciliation** if that is their existing deliverable; choose **Downstream Supply Risk** only when the buyer already produces a distillate/refinery/supply note. Do not force one SKU bundle onto both.

## Chargeable offer (not public list pricing)

**$1,500 fixed / four consecutive weekly release cycles** for one named buyer team, one already-performed question, one scoped SKU bundle, and one parallel evidence pack per cycle. Pilot delivery is operator-managed using the existing founding-pilot access path; it does not require billing software.

Each pack includes a reproducible source-to-claim trail, freshness status, transparent deterministic calculations, and a review-ready summary. It is **decision support, not a price forecast, trading signal, independent data feed, or guarantee of accuracy**. Buyer retains editorial judgment. No bespoke private-data connector, automated client distribution, SLA, or new integration is included.

**Continuation hypothesis:** $750/month for the same managed weekly workflow, to be offered only if four cycles demonstrate buyer value and operating cost is acceptable. No commitment to continuation is implied.

## Why a buyer might pay

The alternative is **free EIA source data plus the buyer's spreadsheet/scripts and checking process**, or a subscription to a broader data/research platform. OilSignal wins only if it compresses recurring labor and produces a traceable, current, reviewable result.

A $1,500 pilot breaks even at **10 hours saved** at a hypothetical $150/hour loaded analyst value, or **2.5 hours per cycle**. That rate is an assumption for discovery, not a claim about buyers' actual economics. At $100/hour the pilot requires 15 hours saved (3.75/cycle). A buyer with a 15-minute weekly workflow is a bad fit regardless of technical interest.

## Qualification and discovery

A qualified account must have **all** of: (1) an existing weekly petroleum release workflow, (2) a named person doing/checking it, (3) a real recipient of the output, and (4) authority or a clear path to a $1,500 pilot decision.

Ask: What exact deliverable goes out after each release? How many minutes/hours from release to client-ready output? Which source checks or date alignments repeat? What happens when data are revised or delayed? Which claim would a client challenge? Which existing tool would this replace or complement? Who could approve a four-cycle $1,500 test? Would an auditable pack with no price forecast solve enough of the work to justify that price?

A demo should use only existing, verifiable OilSignal capabilities. Synthetic fixtures must be visibly marked **DEMO / NOT LIVE EIA DATA**. Do not show synthetic figures as current market facts. If live freshness or a source route cannot be verified, fail closed and say so.

## Evidence collection (do not manufacture results)

Track for each prospect: organization; buyer role; current deliverable; baseline minutes/cycle; source-check count; buyer authority; workflow type (crude/downstream); current alternatives; stated objection; demo completed?; $1,500 offer made?; concrete payment/procurement next step?; date and exact buyer words. Record **not contacted** until actual outreach occurs.

During any paid pilot record per release: expected release week; data freshness/pass-fail; existing-process elapsed minutes; OilSignal-process elapsed minutes; human intervention minutes; source lookups; corrections; claims checked; claims traceable; client-use yes/no; buyer feedback; fulfillment audit identity. Use only comparable weeks and separate software time from human review.

## Falsifiable gates

**Commercial gate:** approach 10 genuinely qualified accounts, hold at least 3 substantive workflow conversations, and obtain at least 1 explicit acceptance of the $1,500 paid pilot or a documented procurement/invoicing step toward it. *Interest, repository stars, a demo request, or an unpriced design partnership do not count.*

**Fail:** zero concrete paid next steps among 10 qualified accounts. Reconsider consultant-vs-operator ICP and packaging before building more features. If repeated objection is “not enough weekly labor,” abandon this wedge. If repeated objection is “needs proprietary inputs,” do not build connectors until a buyer commits economically to that scope.

**Pilot conversion gate:** after four cycles, buyer confirms repeat use and agrees to a paid continuation at a stated price; delivery costs leave acceptable margin. Otherwise stop or repackage. Do not claim recurring revenue without an actual commitment.

## Engineering freeze

No billing/account system, SSO, broad connectors, prediction engine, generic chat, CRM, new data feeds, or dashboard redesign before the commercial gate. Existing deterministic reports, cited SKUs, freshness checks, pilot entitlement and fulfillment audit are the product.

## Operator checklist

1. Verify the selected SKU bundle, current registry, freshness, and source citations before any buyer demo.
2. Prepare a clearly labeled fixture example and one redacted report template.
3. Establish the buyer's baseline before cycle one.
4. Obtain explicit user approval before external outreach, payment arrangements, contracts, or launch.
5. Log outcomes and revisit this offer only after real commercial evidence.

This is an **independent commercial experiment**, not a requirement or blocker for any existing OilSignal engineering pipeline.
